How it works

Walk the site. Animus structures the field event.

Capture the site walk, structure the field events, and turn the day into a daily report draft your team can review, edit, and submit.

The team

Less time behind a screen. More time solving real problems.

Animus was founded by Rozda Askari and Jason Lee, who first met in the Robotics Club at William & Mary. The company is built around a shared belief that the best technology should reduce administrative overhead so people can stay present in the real world and focused on the work that actually matters.

Rozda Askari

Co-founder & CEO LinkedIn (opens in a new tab)

Brings hands-on experience solving complex technology problems at scale across software engineering, cybersecurity, IoT, wireless infrastructure, and enterprise AI. Before co-founding Animus, Rozda worked at Accenture and Verizon, helping organizations adopt and operationalize emerging technologies across government systems, intelligence platforms, Fortune 500 enterprises, and global infrastructure environments. His work has centered on turning fragmented, high-volume signals into actionable systems, a foundation that uniquely positions him to guide the next major shift in enterprise computing: bringing AI agents and smart glasses into the physical workflows where real work happens.
